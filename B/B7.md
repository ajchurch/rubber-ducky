# B7. Participate in CS/DS/cybersecurity clubs’ activity.

Every monday night, I attend the University Computer Club's (UCC) Project Nights. It is a very small thing in the UCC clubroom where people just work on their projects, assignments, and uni work and ask for help where needed. As a committee member, I attend these to also get to know the fellow UCC community. 

I also attend the weekly committee meetings as a member of the UCC committee. Minutes are posted with evidence of my attendence (example one here: https://www.ucc.asn.au/infobase/committee/2026/2026-04-22.ucc)
