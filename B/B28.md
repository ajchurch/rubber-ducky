# B28. Produce a cyber safety flyer for (choose 1): elders, high school students, CEOs, Uni students.

I have decided to make a cyber safety flyer for elders. While a lot of the information can be used for everyone, this specifically targets elders because they are more likely to fall for these types of scams. The ACCC states that Aussies aged 65+ reportedly lose around 100 million dollars a year from scams, where over 62000 scams are reported by this age group yearly.
