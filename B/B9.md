# B9. Participate in an industry-related cybersecurity event.

On Tuesday, May 5th, I attended an industry cybersecurity and enterprise technology event hosted by UCC in partnership with the IBM Z Student Ambassador program. 

The session featured two senior guest speakers from 21CS, an official IBM partner, who provided an introduction to IBM Z mainframe technology.

The event looked at the critical role IBM Z mainframes play in global digital infrastructure, handling approximately 80% of worldwide digital transactions. Given the significant industry demand for mainframe skilled graduates and the current talent shortage, the session highlighted IBM Z as a potential career pathway for students like myself after we graduate. I gained a lot of foundational knowledge of enterprise-grade systems that support large-scale financial, governmental, and corporate operations, which are all systems central to modern cybersecurity architecture due to the sensitive and high volume data they process.
