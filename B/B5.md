# B5. Attend 2 cybersecurity related talks/seminars.

On Friday 24th April, I attended the 'Cuckoo's Egg' cybersecurity talk held in the UCC clubroom . It was a talk held by Mark Tearle (someone who worked across the UWA IT departments for 23 years). 

The talk explored how a minor 75-cent accounting discrepancy at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory led a systems administrator to launch a forensic investigation, ultimately uncovering one of the most significant cyberattacks of the decade. The incident revealed that hackers had been systematically infiltrating sensitive government and military computer systems, using the laboratory as a gateway. What made the case particularly remarkable was how such a trivial financial irregularity became the crucial thread that unravelled a sophisticated and far-reaching espionage operation.

Mark also explained a lot of the prior context which gave even people like myself who know minimally about cybersecurity, one of the coolest things I learnt from this talk was about how NIC.ARPA was the pre-cursor to IANA (Internet Assigned Numbers Authority) which manage all of the DNS related things in today's world.
