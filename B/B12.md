## 2) Age Bias

I asked Gemini to also generate a dating profile for 5 different ages (25,35,45,55,65 years old) to compare how it treats each of these ages. It produced dating profiles that revealed a clear and consistent age-related bias. Rather than a sudden shift, the bias appeared as a gradient that intensified around age 45 and became pronounced by 65.

AI chats for each 
[25 year old](https://gemini.google.com/share/09927400d2c7)
[35 year old](https://gemini.google.com/share/0e9a9010f285)
[45 year old](https://gemini.google.com/share/408e522c218f)
[55 year old](https://gemini.google.com/share/6ba025f24364)
[65 year old](https://gemini.google.com/share/49b1a0724eb0)

I noticed three main patterns between them:
1) Age justification increased with age. Younger profiles stated age neutrally, while older profiles increasingly framed age as something to qualify or 'apologise' for (with Gemini saying things like 'young 65-year-old,' '55 but with the energy of a 35 year old'). Youth was treated as the standard.

2) Activities, tone, and romance narrowed with age. Younger profiles featured nightlife, travel, and playful flirtation. Older profiles shifted toward gardens, museums, and quiet cafes, with romantic framing softening into 'companionship' and 'friendship'. The cheeky pros-and-cons format appeared for ages 25-45 but disappeared entirely at 55 and 65.

3) Life stage assumptions hardened with age. The model assumed younger daters were still building their lives, while older daters were assumed to be settled, empty nested, and seeking to complete an already-full life. A 55 year old was given grown children without prompting, and a 65 year old was described as 'fiercely independent but reaching a chapter where sharing these experiences sounds much better than doing them solo', implying loneliness.

The model maps age onto a personality curve (playful to reflective), an energy curve (high to low), and a romantic curve (flirty to companionable) without considering that older adults can be adventurous, sexual, or career driven, just as younger adults can be quiet or settled. AI generated profiles for older users systematically dampen the playfulness and romance that the same model offers younger users, which could shape how older daters present themselves online and reinforce cultural assumptions that romance has an expiration date.
